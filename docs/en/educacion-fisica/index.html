Physical Education

With Isidora

Your body as a lab: measuring it, understanding it and training it with judgement, looking after it (eating, resting, your back, your mind), knowing what to do if someone collapses, moving better and with others, and looking critically at what sport moves in society. It matters because it is the only subject that teaches you to manage your own health for the rest of your life, and because the decree wants you to finish able to plan your own physical activity.
