Classroom · Year 1 of Bachillerato (upper secondary)

Musical Language and Practice

With Malik

How music is made and how to make it: the layers of a song (rhythm, bass, chords and melody), the rhythms of the world with their exact patterns, harmony from the physics of sound, scales and modes, notation from neumes to today, and creating, improvising and sharing your own. Everything is heard before it is named, seen at the same time in the score and in the sound, and done with free software. It matters because music is a language almost all of us hear every day and almost no one can read from the inside.
