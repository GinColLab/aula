Teacher sheets

Each class of the GinCol Lab channel has its teacher sheet here: objective, the official Spanish curriculum quoted word for word (Royal Decree 243/2022), how the class is built, typical mistakes, questions with their solutions, a plan for a 50-minute lesson and a classroom activity. On the web and as a printable PDF, with a student worksheet without solutions, under a CC BY 4.0 licence. The videos are in Spanish, with English subtitles; the images on the sheets come from the videos and keep their Spanish text. In Spain, the Bachillerato is the last two years of secondary school (ages 16–18).

How to use them

  1. Before class: the video, whole or by chapters; each chapter on the sheet links to its minute.
  2. In class: the 50-minute plan says where to pause the video, what to ask and when to do the activity.
  3. For students: the PDF worksheet, without solutions; the solutions are on the teacher sheet.
  4. Afterwards: the practice questions are corrected at the start of the next class (spaced practice).

How to cite them

GinCol Lab (year). Title of the class. Teacher sheet: subject, year, topic and class. Address of the sheet. CC BY 4.0. Each sheet carries its citation, ready to copy.
