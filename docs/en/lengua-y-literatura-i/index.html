Spanish Language and Literature I

With Vera

The first year of Spanish Language and Literature in the Bachillerato: reading, writing and analysing texts with precision, understanding how the simple sentence works, and travelling through the classics of Spanish literature from the kharjas to Bécquer and Rosalía de Castro. It is the basis of what is asked in year 2 and in the university entrance exam (PAU): commenting on a text, analysing a sentence and placing a work in its tradition.
