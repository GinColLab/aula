Biology, Geology and Environmental Sciences

With Nil

A journey through the planet and through life: what the Earth is like and how it changes, how its history is read, how microorganisms, plants and animals live, and how it all fits into ecosystems that we are now putting to the test. It lays the foundations of the Biology and the Geology and Environmental Sciences of year 2, and teaches you to reason with data about health, climate and sustainability.
