Classroom · Year 1 of Bachillerato (upper secondary)

Artistic Drawing I

With Marina

Learning to look and to draw what you see: measuring with your pencil, blocking in a head, a hand, a body or an animal, shading with real light, composing, putting a space into perspective and using colour, with the great masters' drawings seen up close. And the history of drawing told through its reasons: what changed in each period and why then. It matters because drawing is a way of knowing, and because almost everything that goes wrong when you draw is not in your hand, but in how you look.
